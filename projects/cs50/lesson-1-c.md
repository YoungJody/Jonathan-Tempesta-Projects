# Lesson 1: C

Part of [CS50x](../cs50.md) · **Language:** C · **Topics:** types, conditionals, loops, functions, compiling

## Hello, It's Me
Prompts for the user's name and greets them by name.
**Practiced:** user input, formatted output.

## Mario — harder version
Prints two side-by-side half-pyramids of blocks, separated by a gap, at a height from 1 to 8 chosen by the user. Re-prompts until the input is valid. CS50 also offers a single-pyramid version of this problem; this is the harder one.
**Practiced:** nested loops, aligning output, input validation.

## Credit — harder version
Validates a credit card number with Luhn's algorithm, then identifies it as American Express, Mastercard, or Visa by its length and starting digits, or reports it as invalid. CS50's easier alternative for this slot is Cash, which makes change with a greedy algorithm.
**Practiced:** extracting digits with arithmetic, checksum logic, long integers.
