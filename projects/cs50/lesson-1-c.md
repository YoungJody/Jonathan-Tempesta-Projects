# Lesson 1: C

Part of [CS50x](../cs50.md) · **Language:** C · **Topics:** types, conditionals, loops, functions, compiling

## Hello, It's Me
Prompts for the user's name and greets them by name.
**Practiced:** user input, formatted output.

## Mario (more comfortable)
Prints two side-by-side half-pyramids of blocks, separated by a gap, at a height from 1 to 8 chosen by the user. Re-prompts until the input is valid.
**Practiced:** nested loops, input validation.

## Credit
Validates a credit card number with Luhn's algorithm, then identifies it as American Express, Mastercard, or Visa by its length and starting digits, or reports it as invalid.
**Practiced:** extracting digits with arithmetic, checksum logic, long integers.
